# public/img

The site's own artwork. Anything here is served from `/img/…`.

`logo.png` is the mark in the top bar, shown 44 px tall. It is cut out of the
drawing by flood-filling the background from the border — white, in this one —
which is kept tight on purpose: the drawing is pale throughout, and a looser cut
eats the cream of the lion and the light around the rider. If the file ever
fails to load, the bar falls back to a plain pin.

`logo-dark.png` is the earlier version, in deeper colours on a black ground.
It is kept in case it is wanted back; swapping them is a rename.

`icon-32.png` is the browser-tab icon and `icon-180.png` the one iOS uses on a
home screen. Both are cut from the same drawing, minus the rider, who stands
well above the lion: keeping her only shrinks everything else until the icon is
a smudge. What is left — the lion and the sabre — is nearly square and fills it.
Both sit on the bar's own dark ground rather than being transparent, because
this artwork is too pale to read against a light browser tab.
